# New archive storage offering and price update

Research Computing will deploy a new archive storage tier in fall 2026. Also, research group storage will not increase in price for calendar year 2027.

<!-- more -->

## Archive storage

RCC will deploy [archive storage](../../storage/archive-storage.md) in fall 2026. This new tier of storage will serve as archive for research data that must be retained long-term. The new archive storage will keep redundant copies of all data, similar to research group storage. MCW investigators will pay an annual fee of $50/TB/year. We will use the same proration and renewal schedule as research group storage.

## Research group storage fee update

RCC has decided to hold the fee for research group storage in calendar year 2027 at $100/TB/year.

2025 - $80/TB/year  
2026 - $100/TB/year  
2027 - {~~$120~>$100~~}/TB/year
